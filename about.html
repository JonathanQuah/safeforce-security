Institutional Longevity

A Proven Legacy of Corporate Defense

Continuous evolution to meet the changing threats of modern commerce, from physical guarding to comprehensive threat elimination.

August 1982

Security Control Founded

Established as Security Control (Fire & Burglary) Sdn Bhd, answering Malaysia's early commercial demand for specialized asset protection, physical night watches, and fire security management.

June 1999

Transition to Safe Force

Transitioned into a full-fledged, highly specialized corporate security provider under the registered corporate name Safe Force (M) Sdn Bhd, adding armed protection, tactical escort, and elite management.

Datuk Hj Ujad bin Abu Bakar

Managing Director

Datuk Hj Ujad brings over two decades of distinguished experience in security, enforcement, and public service. Building on his strong foundation in discipline, leadership, and operational management, he transitioned into the private sector to lead Safe Force (M) Sdn Bhd with strategic oversight, governance, and operational excellence.
